About Us

The Data Quality Department is responsible for ensuring the accuracy, integrity, consistency, and reliability of data that supports university philanthropy and advancement efforts. Through careful stewardship, collaboration, and continuous improvement, we help enable trusted reporting, meaningful donor engagement, and informed decision-making in support of the university’s philanthropic mission.
