Alumni Survey

Alumni Questionnaires are one of our main ways of keeping in touch with alumni and keeping contact information current. Surveys go out every month except December and January, aiming to land in the mail during the third full week. Data Services sends the file for next month's questionnaire around the 1st, and the formatted file is due to Impressions Direct two weeks before the mail date.

See the Alumni Survey Schedule workbook for which reunion years are up each month and how many are mailed.

Slate Integration

Slate is the system of record for Undergraduate Admissions and feeds new University families into ARCH once a deposit is made — pulled in weekly rather than live, since ARCH's dashboards don't handle a live feed well. Slate only creates a new Account or Contact when one doesn't already exist, matched on name, contact info, and (for students) their University Admissions ID. If a student drops or defers, their record — and their parent's, unless that parent already has another WashU relationship on file — comes back out of ARCH.

Keeping Parent & Student Data Clean

The same cleanup pass runs on every record Slate brings in — for parents and students alike — before it's considered done. Married parents are joined into a household, duplicates are merged, and relationships Slate can't infer on its own (like a parent's spouse, or a sibling the student mentioned) get validated and added by hand.

  • Clean up capitalization and spelling
  • Join married parents into a single household
  • Merge duplicate accounts
  • Remove duplicate education records
  • Manually add and validate relationships and inverse relationships
